Why the United States first. Waymo runs about 500,000 paid rides a week in 10 cities, roughly 200 million driverless miles a year: that is where real driverless claims are. Tesla's supervised driving system has 8 billion miles on Tesla's own figures: volume for screening, but liability sits mostly with the driver, so we sell triage there, not guaranteed recovery. The US publishes crash reports under a federal reporting order, which the regulator says may be incomplete, and public real-crash studies to validate against: our simulator reproduces 88 percent of crashes and 94 percent of near-crashes in the 100-Car study. The United Kingdom has no vehicle on the government's self-driving list yet, so little volume until its regime is live, expected late 2027 from secondary reporting.

Walk the six steps. Intake: the crash bundle arrives and passes an integrity gate; tampering, clock drift or log gaps stop the case. Liability: who was in control, the software, the handover, any faults. Simulate: the simulator runs two published reference drivers, A and B, and checks it can reproduce the reported crash. Price: recoverable money as a range after legal cost. Outcome: one of eight, with named next steps, an owner and the limitation deadline first. Sign-off: a named human approves the spend. Then it loops: new evidence re-runs the claim and a ledger shows what changed. Code produces every number; the agents only challenge it.

Agents never produce numbers; the finance engine does.

How the agents interact for long runs. The portfolio lead takes the next claim from the queue and hands it to the investigator and the reconstructor in parallel, then to the counterfactual analyst, then calls the insurance engine and records a finding, writing a portfolio note every three claims. It reuses the same three worker threads for every claim. Long runs stay safe through a queue with a budget cap, reused workers, a limit of five reconstruction attempts after which the claim is flagged low confidence rather than forced, a debate in which an adjuster prices the claim, defence counsel attacks it and the adjuster rebuts (a claim is marked CONTESTED for a human if a rejected challenge would flip the decision), and a trace and ledger of every run. Agents never produce numbers; the finance engine recomputes every figure.

Three ways to charge. Per claim: 300 pounds (about 400 dollars) for a deep analysis of a driverless claim and 75 for a driver-assist screen; suits pilots. A success fee of 10 percent of recovery undercuts the 15 to 33 percent recovery firms keep and aligns incentives, but the money arrives years later. A subscription of 40 to 80 thousand pounds a year suits operators, insurers and managing general agents and is the one that scales, because per-claim alone is too small before 2030. The AI run costs 27 cents per claim, measured including the debate; human review is about 99 percent of cost and stays for responsible design. Gross margin about 87 to 90 percent. Plan: 240 thousand pounds of revenue in 2027 rising to 2.22 million in 2029, break-even 2029, on a 2 million pound seed. All assumptions to test with the first customer.

Name the risks before the judges do. The driverless-only market is small before 2030: United States first, subscriptions across the chain, driver-assist screening for volume. Our engine models United Kingdom law today; a United States legal and cost model is still to be built with counsel, and the liability test and simulator carry over. The operator holds the evidence: the desk routes to a preservation letter and a court disclosure request and shows what better evidence would change. The reference models are guidance, not law: always quote careful drivers A and B together, flag model-dependent answers, and a human signs off. Misuse: a triage number must never be used to deny or pressure a victim; victims are paid before any recovery decision and outputs are triage evidence, never a liability finding. Built in: ranges not points, refusal to guess, humans decide, an evidence integrity gate, an audit trail, synthetic data only.
