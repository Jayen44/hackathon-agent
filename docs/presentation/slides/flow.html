Automated Vehicle Recovery Desk · Product and demo

Walk left to right. Evidence arrives as a crash bundle and passes an integrity gate. The liability engine checks who was in control, the software, the handover and any faults. The simulator then asks whether a careful, competent driver, using the two published reference models in United Nations Regulation 157, would have avoided the crash. Finance prices the recovery as a range after legal cost. Every claim ends in one of eight outcomes with next steps. The key line: code makes every number, agents only challenge. Scoring cue: architecture (agent design 15%), demo clarity.
