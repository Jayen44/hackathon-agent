Every claim ends in one of eight outcomes, from pursue to close.

Walk the six steps. Intake: the crash bundle arrives and passes an integrity gate; tampering, clock drift or log gaps stop the case. Liability: who was in control, the software, the handover, any faults. Simulate: the simulator runs two published reference drivers, A and B, and checks it can reproduce the reported crash. Price: recoverable money as a range after legal cost. Outcome: one of eight, with named next steps, an owner and the limitation deadline first. Sign-off: a named human approves the spend. Then it loops: new evidence re-runs the claim and a ledger shows what changed. Code produces every number; the agents only challenge it.
