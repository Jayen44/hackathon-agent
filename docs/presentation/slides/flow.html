The eight outcomes: pursue, pull logs, needs inputs, human review, settle without recovery, recover from insured, motor claim, close. Automated Vehicle Recovery Desk

Walk the six steps. Intake: the crash bundle arrives and passes an integrity gate; tampering, clock drift or log gaps stop the case. Liability: who was in control, the software version, the handover, any faults. Careful driver: the simulator runs two published reference drivers, A and B, and checks it can reproduce the reported crash. Pricing: recoverable money as a range after legal cost. Outcome: one of eight, with named next steps, an owner and the limitation deadline first. Sign-off: a named human approves the spend.
