Sources: Swiss Re and Waymo study (25.3 million miles); Arbitration Forums 2025 (all auto claims).

Driverless vehicles crash far less often: one insurer study found 88 percent fewer property-damage and 92 percent fewer injury claims than human drivers, so the few claims left are contested and high-stakes. The evidence is held by the party you would be chasing. Recovery is a large market: about 27 billion dollars of US subrogation demands in 2025 across all auto claims, with recovery firms keeping 15 to 33 percent. Two mistakes cost money: chasing a losing claim and missing a winning one. A fast, evidenced first read prevents both.
