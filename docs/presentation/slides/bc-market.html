Business case, part 5: the honest sizing, from market/MARKET.md. In 2030 the base-case total addressable market for per-claim work is about 7.7 million dollars a year, with a range from 1.4 to 45 million depending on adoption. The serviceable slice is about 1.2 million, and a 20 percent share is about a quarter of a million. That is too small on its own. The business works if we add subscriptions across the liability chain (operators, their insurers, managing general agents) and use driver-assist claims for volume now. The United Kingdom adds almost nothing until its regime is live in late 2027. Every share, price and rate is an assumption. Scoring cue: business viability (20%).
