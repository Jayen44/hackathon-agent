Close with where we are and the ask. Working now: 22 example crashes all reproduced by the simulator, two agent systems, a live dashboard and over 200 automated tests. Not yet: all crash data is synthetic, there is no United States legal or cost model, and no tested price. The ask: a 2 million pound seed, one insurer or operator for a United States pilot on settled claims, and one United States counsel to shape the cost model. Likely questions: is this legal today (we start in the US; the UK regime is not live); why trust the number (published reference models, a range with A and B together, a human signs off); what if the operator will not share logs (preservation letter and court disclosure request).
