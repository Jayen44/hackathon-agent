Working now: 22 example crashes all reproduced by the simulator, two agent systems, a live dashboard and one-command run, deadline clocks, an audit ledger and over 200 automated tests. Not yet: all crash data is synthetic, there is no United States legal or cost model, and no real insurer data or price test. The ask: a 2 million pound seed, one insurer or operator for a United States pilot on settled claims, and one United States counsel to shape the cost model.
