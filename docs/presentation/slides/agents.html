The agent team

Four agents work the queue as a team

Portfolio lead

Picks the claim, delegates, records the finding, writes a portfolio note every three claims

Investigator

Reads the bundle: facts, evidence tier, unknowns and red flags

Reconstructor

Checks the simulator reproduces the crash; revises ranges, five attempts at most

Counterfactual analyst

Chance of avoiding the crash for two reference drivers, then one evidence tier up

Hand-offs, retry limits, a budget cap and a full trace per run. Automated Vehicle Recovery Desk · Agents and safeguards

The fleet is a managed agent team. The lead takes the next claim, sends the investigator and reconstructor off in parallel, then asks the counterfactual analyst for the careful-driver result, then calls the insurance engine. Error recovery is built in: if the simulator does not reproduce the crash, the reconstructor revises ranges it can justify from the bundle, at most five attempts, then reports low confidence rather than forcing a match. A budget cap and a trace record every run. Scoring cue: agent design and architecture (15%): planning, hand-offs, memory, error recovery.
