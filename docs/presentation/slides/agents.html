How the agents interact for long runs. The portfolio lead takes the next claim from the queue and hands it to the investigator and the reconstructor in parallel, then to the counterfactual analyst, then calls the insurance engine and records a finding, writing a portfolio note every three claims. It reuses the same three worker threads for every claim. Long runs stay safe through a queue with a budget cap, reused workers, a limit of five reconstruction attempts after which the claim is flagged low confidence rather than forced, a debate in which an adjuster prices the claim, defence counsel attacks it and the adjuster rebuts (a claim is marked CONTESTED for a human if a rejected challenge would flip the decision), and a trace and ledger of every run. Agents never produce numbers; the finance engine recomputes every figure.
