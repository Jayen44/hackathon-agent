Business case, part 2: worked unit economics, all computed by the finance engine, not estimated by hand. Below roughly ten thousand pounds even a near-certain win can lose money if it goes to trial, so the right move is a pre-action letter only. From about twenty-five thousand the worst case turns positive and the claim is worth pursuing. Above one hundred thousand there is no fixed-cost regime and no sourced figure, so we refuse to price it until counsel supplies a budget. That refusal is a feature.
