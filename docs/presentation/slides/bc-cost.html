Business case, part 1: where the value comes from. Two errors, both expensive. Chasing a doomed claim burns legal cost: roughly one to twelve thousand pounds on a small claim, nine to fifty-one thousand on a sixty thousand pound claim, using the fixed recoverable costs tables from the Civil Procedure Rules, from a pre-issue settlement up to a one-day trial; real spend can be higher and adverse costs follow a loss. Missing a good claim loses all of it, especially after the two-year limit. The desk is a triage layer that stops the first and catches the second. Scoring cue: business viability (20%): a real problem.
