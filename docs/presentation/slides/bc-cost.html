Business case, part 1: where the value comes from. The recovery market is large: about 27 billion dollars of subrogation demands were filed in the US in 2025 across all auto claims, and recovery firms keep 15 to 33 percent, which tells you both that the money is real and that there is a middleman to undercut. Driverless vehicles have 88 percent fewer property-damage and 92 percent fewer injury claims, so the few that remain are contested and high-stakes: exactly where a good triage decision matters. Two errors cost money: chasing a doomed claim, and missing a good one. Scoring cue: business viability (20%): a real problem.
