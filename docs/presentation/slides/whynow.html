Where we start

United States first; the United Kingdom when its regime is live

United States, today

Driverless fleets already run at scale: about 500,000 paid rides a week for one operator, and about 200 million driverless miles a year.

Start here with recovery claims.

United Kingdom, today

No vehicle is on the government list of self-driving vehicles, so the 2018 Act’s insurer-pays-first route does not yet apply. The London pilot has a safety driver.

Routes today: contribution, or the Consumer Protection Act.

United Kingdom, from late 2027

Automated Vehicles Act 2024 authorisation regime expected, with “careful and competent driver” as the benchmark.

The desk is built for it: a regime field switches to authorised.

Sources: operator figures and market sizing in market/MARKET.md; gov.uk list of self-driving vehicles; Automated Vehicles Act 2024. UK timing is from secondary reporting.

Positioning decision from the team: start with United States recovery claims, where driverless fleets already operate at scale, and be ready for the United Kingdom when its Automated Vehicles Act 2024 regime is in force.
