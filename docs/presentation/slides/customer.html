Target market. The buyer is not one team. Insurers of driverless fleets and their reinsurers need to know whether to pursue recovery. Operators need the same analysis to defend themselves and review incidents. Fleet managing general agents sit between them. Day to day the users are claims handlers, recoveries counsel and managers who sign the spend. The driverless-only claim market is about 7.7 million dollars a year by 2030 in the base case, range 1.4 to 45 million, so on its own it is small; we sell across the chain and use driver-assist claims, about 15 to 20 times more numerous today, as the volume wedge. The 27 billion dollar figure is all auto subrogation, the market we sit inside. Scoring cue: clear customer (business viability).
