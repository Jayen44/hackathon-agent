Automated Vehicle Recovery Desk · Problem and customer

The buyer is a UK motor insurer that covers self-driving vehicles. The users are the people who handle recoveries: handlers, recoveries counsel and the manager who signs the spend. Every file raises the same four questions, and today answering them takes expert reconstruction and legal time before anyone knows whether the claim is worth it. Scoring cue: clear customer (business viability).
