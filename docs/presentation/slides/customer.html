The buyer is not one team: insurers of driverless fleets and their reinsurers, robotaxi operators for defence and incident review, and fleet managing general agents. Day to day the users are claims handlers, recoveries counsel and the managers who sign the spend. The driverless-only market is about 7.7 million dollars a year by 2030 in the base case, range 1.4 to 45 million: too small alone, so we sell across the chain and use driver-assist claims, about 15 to 20 times more numerous today, for volume. The 27 billion dollar figure is all auto subrogation, the market we sit inside.
