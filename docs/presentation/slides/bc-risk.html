Name the risks before the judges do. First, the driverless-only market is small before 2030: the answer is United States first, subscriptions across the liability chain, and driver-assist screening for volume. Second, and this is our own gap: the engine models United Kingdom law today, and a United States legal and cost model is still to be built with counsel; the liability test and the simulator carry over. Third, the evidence is held by the other side, so the desk routes to a preservation letter and a disclosure request. Fourth, the reference models are guidance, so always quote drivers A and B together. Fifth, driver-assist liability sits mainly with the driver, so we sell triage, not guaranteed recovery.
