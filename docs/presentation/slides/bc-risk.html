Automated Vehicle Recovery Desk · Business case

Name the risks before the judges do. Legal basis: flagged on every claim and confirmed by counsel. Evidence access: there is no right to the logs, so the desk routes to a preservation letter and a pre-action disclosure request and shows what a better evidence tier would change. Model credibility: the reference models are guidance, so we run two as a bracket and say when the answer is model-dependent. Small claims: pre-action only below about twenty-five thousand pounds. Competition: we are the triage layer before loss adjusters and reconstruction firms spend; we hand them a ranked file. Scoring cues: business viability, responsible design.
