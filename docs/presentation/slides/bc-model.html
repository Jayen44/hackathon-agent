Business case: three ways to charge. Per claim: three hundred pounds for a deep analysis of a driverless claim and seventy-five for a driver-assist screen; suits pilots and occasional customers. A success fee of 10 percent of recovery undercuts the 15 to 33 percent that recovery firms keep and aligns incentives, but the money arrives years later. A platform subscription of 40 to 80 thousand pounds a year suits operators, insurers and managing general agents, and is the scalable one because per-claim alone is too small before 2030. Unit costs: the AI run costs 27 cents per claim, measured in our prototype including the debate; the real cost is an hour of human review, about 99 percent of the total, which we keep for responsible design. Gross margin about 87 to 90 percent. The plan: 240 thousand pounds of revenue in 2027 rising to 2.22 million in 2029 with break-even in 2029, on a 2 million pound seed, all assumptions that the first customer will test. Scoring cue: business viability (20%).
