Business case, part 3. Three structures. Per claim: three hundred pounds for a deep analysis of a driverless claim, and seventy-five for a driver-assist screen. A success fee of 10 percent of recovery undercuts the 15 to 33 percent that recovery firms keep, because we are the triage and evidence layer, not litigation counsel. A platform subscription of 40 to 80 thousand pounds a year suits operators, insurers and fleet managing general agents. Unit costs: the AI run costs 27 cents per claim, measured in our prototype including the debate; the real cost is an hour of human review, about 99 percent of the total, which we keep because responsible design needs a human in the loop. Gross margin about 87 to 90 percent. The prices are assumptions to be tested with the first customer. Scoring cue: business viability (20%).
