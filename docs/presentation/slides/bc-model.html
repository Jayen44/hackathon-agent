Three ways to charge. Per claim: 300 pounds (about 400 dollars) for a deep analysis of a driverless claim and 75 for a driver-assist screen; suits pilots. A success fee of 10 percent of recovery undercuts the 15 to 33 percent recovery firms keep and aligns incentives, but the money arrives years later. A subscription of 40 to 80 thousand pounds a year suits operators, insurers and managing general agents and is the one that scales, because per-claim alone is too small before 2030. The AI run costs 27 cents per claim, measured including the debate; human review is about 99 percent of cost and stays for responsible design. Gross margin about 87 to 90 percent. Plan: 240 thousand pounds of revenue in 2027 rising to 2.22 million in 2029, break-even 2029, on a 2 million pound seed. All assumptions to test with the first customer.
