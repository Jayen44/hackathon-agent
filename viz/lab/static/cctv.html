2Calibrate the road plane

Don't know W? typical UK trunk-road lane: an assumption you must confirm on site or from a plan.

σ defaults are design assumptions; they set the calibration part of every error bar. Calibrate a rectangle that covers where the vehicles drive: extrapolating far beyond it widens the speed error.
